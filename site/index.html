01 / Performance

Measured, not promised.

Same Groth16.
Less waiting.

The core proof stays compact. The work behind it gets faster: smaller quotient FFTs, parallel MSMs, and fewer inversions.

02 / Under the hood

Built to be inspected.

The details
make the difference.

A familiar proving system, with practical improvements where they count.

01

A leaner prover

An n-point quotient coset, running-product scalars, and parallel multiscalar multiplication.

↗ 02

Stricter verification

Identity-point rejection, input validation, and explicit checks for unsupported proof variants.

↗ 03

One batch check

Fiat–Shamir challenges bind the key, statements, and proofs into a single multi-pairing check.

↗ 04

Tools around the core

Poseidon and Merkle circuits, range checks, plus Solidity and WASM verifier generation.

↗
From the research bench

Lookups, folding, polynomial commitments, and a post-quantum scaffold live alongside the core. Each has its own assumptions and limits.
